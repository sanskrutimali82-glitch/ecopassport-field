Environmental Insights

Simple ways to make your routine more sustainable.

🚲 Greener travel
Walk, cycle or use public transport for short journeys.
💡 Save electricity
Switch off lights and devices when not in use.
💧 Conserve water
Close taps while brushing and fix leaks.
♻️ Reduce and reuse
Carry a reusable bottle and separate recyclable waste.
🌱 Grow green spaces
Plant and care for native trees and plants.
🥗 Reduce food waste
Plan meals and make use of leftovers.
